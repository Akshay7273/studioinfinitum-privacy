Privacy Policy for Asteroid Tether Drift

Package Name: com.studio100.day020.asteroidtetherdrift

Asteroid Tether Drift operates 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. Campaign progress, mission stars, best times, and settings (sound, reduced motion, controls) are saved locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is cleared if you uninstall the application or clear app data.

The game makes zero network requests, uses no third-party SDKs, no advertisements, and no analytics. Audio is generated locally with WebAudio synth sounds and can be muted in settings. The Android build declares the VIBRATE permission.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact Studio 100 Support at privacy@studio100apps.in.
